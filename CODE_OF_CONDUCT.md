# Code of Conduct

VidSearch adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@daven-c](https://github.com/daven-c), privately via GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
